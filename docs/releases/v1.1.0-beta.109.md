# Core 1.1.0 beta109

A conversation loop restarts its goal.

- When a session loop fires and the conversation's goal is paused (the agent
  reported it was blocked until the next firing, or it used up its
  continuations), the goal is reactivated with a fresh continuation budget
  before the turn runs. A standing role, such as a daily triage, now works
  under its goal every day. An achieved goal stays achieved.
